1. Strict Project SLA & Mandatory 2-Day Delivery Rule

To ensure high operational speed and exceptional client satisfaction across the platform, Fort Developers / Fort Graphics enforces a strict Service Level Agreement (SLA) regarding project fulfillment timeframes.

Mandatory SLA Rule: Every project, job order, digital asset commission, or development task collected by a designer or developer through this platform MUST be completed and delivered within exactly two (2) calendar days (48 hours) from the precise moment of task acceptance or assignment, unless an explicit, documented time extension is authorized prior to task acceptance.

Failure to meet the strict 2-day delivery window without an approved extension will result in an automatic flag on the designer's internal reliability index, potential reassignment of the project to a secondary developer, forfeiture of priority job routing, and potential financial penalties including project cancellation. Repeated failure to adhere to the 2-day fulfillment timeline will result in permanent termination of the designer's or developer's platform access credentials.

2. Client Satisfaction & Escrow Authorization Protocol

The platform operates an escrow-style protection framework designed to guarantee quality assurance and protect the fiscal interests of project owners (clients).

  • Conditional Payment Hold: Upon commissioning a project, client funds are securely escrowed by the platform. No funds shall be disbursed, released, or paid out to the designer or developer until the project owner explicitly approves the delivered work to his or her complete satisfaction.
  • Review and Revisions: The client retains the right to inspect delivered assets, request necessary revisions, and verify that all pre-agreed design or development specifications have been fully met.
  • Dispute Resolution: If a project owner reasonably demonstrates that the delivered work fails to meet the project brief or agreed standards, the funds will remain locked in escrow until the designer rectifies all highlighted deficiencies. If satisfaction cannot be achieved, the platform administrators reserve the right to process a full or partial refund to the project owner.

3. Maximum Active Project Limits (Capacity Constraints)

To prevent workflow overload, burnout, and quality degradation across graphic design and technical development jobs, the platform strictly enforces a concurrency constraint on project ownership.

Concurrency Rule: No individual designer, developer, or agency account is permitted to hold, accept, or work on more than two (2) active projects simultaneously at any single point in time.

Our automated dispatch systems will automatically lock a designer's queue once they reach the limit of two active, unapproved projects. A designer or developer will only become eligible to collect or accept a new project once one or both of their active projects have been formally approved, finalized, and closed out by the respective project owner(s).

4. Financial Revenue Split & Price Increase Incentive Model

Fort Developers / Fort Graphics operates a transparent revenue-sharing framework designed to heavily favor the creative talent and technical experts delivering value on our platform.

4.1 Revenue Sharing Ratio

All gross revenues, project fees, and transaction sums collected for completed and approved projects shall be split strictly on a 33.3% to 77.7% ratio basis:

  • 77.7% Share: Disbursed directly to the active Designer or Developer responsible for fulfilling the project (representing the higher majority share).
  • 33.3% Share: Retained by Fort Developers / Fort Graphics to cover platform maintenance, server infrastructure, payment gateway processing, customer support operations, and platform marketing.

4.2 Designer-Driven Price Increments & Earnings Expansion

Designers and developers possess the operational leverage to adjust or increment base pricing on specialized project tasks, complex scopes, or custom visual briefs presented to customers.

Direct Income Correlation: Any approved increment in the final project price quoted to and paid by the customer will directly increase the absolute net income earned by the designer for that project. Because the 77.7% payout ratio applies to the total agreed-upon project cost, any upward pricing negotiation directly inflates the designer’s financial yield per project.
